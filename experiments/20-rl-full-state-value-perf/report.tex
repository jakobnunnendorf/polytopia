\section{Question and hypothesis}
\textbf{The idea (David, 26 Nov 2026, issue \#166).} Experiment 17 tuned the gate's greedy bots for Perfection, and
\texttt{greedy-full-probe-perf} is now the learner's base bot for both tracks and the frozen anchor. Re-run experiment
16 on top of it, so the value function describes the bot the RL agent will steer. Method unchanged: the state is six
exact integers (turn, cities, income, units, sum of city levels, techs), no bins; $V(s_t) = \mathbb{E}[\text{final
score} - \text{score}_t \mid s_t]$, the remaining score of the bot's own play.

\textbf{Prediction} (before the run; from experiment 16, adjusted for a bot tuned for this mode):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Coverage stays high:} 95--98\% of held-out learner turns land in a state seen in the 300{,}000
        training games (16: 97.0\%).
  \item \emph{Same overall picture:} model-based $V$ is 9--14\% below the turn baseline in RMSE over all held-out
        turns (16: 11.9\%), and 22--30\% below it at turns 20--25 (16: 27.6\% and 25.9\%).
  \item \emph{Same split between the estimators:} Monte Carlo is better in the early turns, the model-based $V$ in
        the late turns, with the crossover between turns 12 and 19 (16: turn 16).
  \item \emph{The bot does slightly better:} mean held-out final score 6{,}800--7{,}000 (16: 6{,}747; the gate's
        baseline on the evaluation seeds: 6{,}839), eliminations 24--30\% (16: 27.6\%).
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Against experiments 13 and 16:} if the model-based $V$ is not below the turn baseline or the estimator split
reverses, their result was specific to the untuned bot.

\section{Mechanism}
As experiment 13 (\texttt{rl-value collect} and \texttt{fit}, \texttt{engine/rlvalue}): the state is read at the start
of each learner turn; reward = change in raw score; three estimators on the same training games: model-based
(certainty equivalence: transitions $(r, s')$ per state, backward induction from the last turn), Monte Carlo
(mean return-to-go per state) and the turn baseline (mean return-to-go per turn). A held-out state not seen in
training gets the turn baseline. New in the tool: the terminal row records whether the learner won, so the report
gives the win rate.
